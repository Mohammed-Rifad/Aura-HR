# 1. Introduction

## 1.1 Project Name

AURA HR – AI-Powered Enterprise Workforce Platform

---

## 1.2 Project Description

AURA HR is a next-generation Human Resource Management System (HRMS) that combines traditional HR operations with Agentic Artificial Intelligence.

Unlike conventional HR software that relies heavily on manual navigation and repetitive administrative tasks, AURA HR introduces specialized AI agents capable of assisting employees, HR professionals, recruiters, managers, and executives through intelligent conversations and workflow automation.

The platform is designed to improve operational efficiency while ensuring humans remain in control of critical business decisions.

---

# 2. Business Background

Most organizations today use multiple disconnected systems for managing employees.

Examples include

• Attendance System

• Leave Portal

• Payroll Software

• Recruitment Software

• Employee Documents

• HR Policies

• Email Systems

• Spreadsheets

As a result

- Information becomes fragmented.
- Employees waste time finding information.
- HR performs repetitive administrative work.
- Managers spend excessive time approving requests.
- Executives lack real-time business insights.

---

# 3. Business Objectives

The primary objective of AURA HR is to centralize all HR operations into one intelligent platform.

Business goals include:

- Improve employee experience.
- Reduce repetitive HR tasks.
- Automate document generation.
- Improve recruitment efficiency.
- Enhance compliance management.
- Enable AI-assisted decision making.
- Increase organizational productivity.
