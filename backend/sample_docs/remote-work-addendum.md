# AURA HR — Remote Work Addendum

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed probation may request a fully remote
arrangement. Requests are reviewed by HR every six months.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop, an external monitor and a headset to fully
remote employees. Equipment remains company property and must be returned
within 14 days of the last working day.

Employees may claim up to 1000 towards a desk and chair, once every three
years, with an itemised receipt.
