# AURA HR — Leave Policy

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees are entitled to 30 calendar days of annual leave per
year, accrued at 2.5 days per completed month of service.

Employees on probation may not take annual leave, but continue to accrue it.

A maximum of 10 unused annual leave days may be carried forward into the
following calendar year. Carried-forward days must be used by 31 March or
they are forfeited.

## Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 15 days of paid sick leave per year.

A medical certificate is required for any sick leave of three consecutive
days or more. Sick leave taken immediately before or after annual leave
always requires a certificate, regardless of length.

## Applying for Leave

Annual leave must be applied for at least 7 days in advance. Requests go to
the employee's direct manager, who must approve or reject within 3 working
days.

Leave of more than 10 consecutive days additionally requires HR approval.

## Unpaid Leave

Unpaid leave may be granted at the company's discretion once annual leave is
exhausted. It does not accrue further leave and is capped at 30 days per year.
