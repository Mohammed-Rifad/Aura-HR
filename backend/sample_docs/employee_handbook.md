# AURA HR — Employee Handbook

## Probation Period

New employees serve a probation period of six months from their joining date.
During probation, either party may terminate employment with seven days'
written notice.

Probation may be extended once, by up to three months, if performance
concerns are documented and shared with the employee in writing before the
original probation end date.

## Notice Period

After confirmation, employees must give 60 days' written notice of
resignation. Employees at manager level or above must give 90 days.

The company may waive part of the notice period at its discretion. Unused
annual leave may be encashed against notice only with written HR approval.

## Working Hours

Standard working hours are 09:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour
unpaid lunch break. The standard working week is 40 hours.

Employees are expected to be present during the core hours of 11:00 to 16:00.
Start and finish times outside core hours may be varied by agreement with the
employee's manager.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval.
Fully remote arrangements require HR approval and are reviewed every six
months.

Remote workers must be reachable during core hours and must attend the office
for scheduled team meetings, performance reviews, and onboarding sessions.

## Public Holidays

The company observes 11 public holidays each calendar year, published every
December for the year ahead.

Where a public holiday falls on a weekend, the following working day is
granted in lieu. Employees required to work on a public holiday receive a
compensatory day off within 30 days.

## Maternity and Paternity Leave

Female employees are entitled to 60 days of paid maternity leave, which may
begin up to 30 days before the expected date of delivery.

Male employees are entitled to 5 days of paid paternity leave, to be taken
within one month of the birth.

Both are in addition to annual leave and do not affect annual leave accrual.

## Performance Reviews

Formal performance reviews take place twice a year, in June and December.

Each review covers goals set at the previous review, an overall rating from 1
to 5, and goals for the coming period. Ratings of 1 or 2 trigger a documented
performance improvement plan lasting 90 days.

Salary revisions are decided in the December cycle and take effect from 1 April.

## Expense Claims

Business expenses must be claimed within 30 days of being incurred. Claims
require an itemised receipt; card statements alone are not accepted.

Claims up to 500 are approved by the employee's manager. Claims above 500
additionally require finance approval. Travel bookings must be made through
the company travel desk wherever possible.

## Grievance Procedure

An employee with a workplace grievance should first raise it informally with
their direct manager.

If unresolved after 10 working days, or if the grievance concerns the manager
themselves, it may be raised formally in writing with HR. HR must acknowledge
within 3 working days and conclude the investigation within 30 days.

## Data and Confidentiality

Employees must not share salary information, personal data of colleagues, or
client information outside the company.

Access to HR systems is granted on a least-privilege basis. Managers may view
records for their own reports only; HR and Admin roles may view all records.
